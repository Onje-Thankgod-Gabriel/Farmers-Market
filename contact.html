Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a farmer?

To register as a farmer, simply click the "Register" button in the top right corner. Select "Farmer" as your role, fill in your details, and submit. Once verified, you can start listing your produce immediately.

How can I buy products on FarmConnect?

Browse the Marketplace section, search for the produce you need, and contact the farmer directly. You can place orders and arrange delivery through our trusted logistics partners.

How do logistics providers join?

Logistics providers can register on the platform by selecting "Logistics" during sign-up. Complete your company profile, and our team will verify your credentials before listing your services.

How are farmers verified?

We verify farmers through a combination of documentation, farm visits, and community feedback. Our verification process ensures trust and authenticity across the platform.

How do I report an issue?

You can report any issue through the "Report" button on product or farmer profiles. Alternatively, use the contact form below, and our support team will respond within 24 hours.
